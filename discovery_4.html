IDEAL OPERATING STATE: THE BALANCE OF DUAL TRUTHS

Descriptive Truth

It reflects statistical reality, acknowledging the presence of bias in data and models. It is the "is" of the world, where we recognize the imperfections and biases that exist.

Transparent Architecture

AI should have the 'Right to Explain,' showing users both the data reality and the ethical path forward.
By applying Bayesian updating, we balance what the world is with what it should be dynamically, allowing for continuous learning and adaptation in the pursuit of social equity.
