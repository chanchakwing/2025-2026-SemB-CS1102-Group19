What if an AI manages the hiring process...
...and it is unfair?

In 2014, Amazon built an AI to find top talent. By 2015, they realized the system had a dangerous flaw: The system learned to penalize female candidates... (Kodiyan, 2019).

The Temptations and Traps of AI Recruiting

The Goal: Reducing the time cost of manually screening thousands of resumes (Time-to-hire).
The Mechanism: AI learning patterns from successful applicants over the past 10 years.
The Flaw: Because the tech industry has been male-dominated for the past 10 years, AI incorrectly associates "male traits" with "success."
