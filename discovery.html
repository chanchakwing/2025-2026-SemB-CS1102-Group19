Research Discoveries Overview

This research summarizes three core findings regarding LLM Social Equity, demonstrating the complete process from identifying problems to implementing technical solutions.

Discovery 1: Verification of Gender Bias

Confirmed that DeepSeek V3.2 exhibits "micro-biases" in persuasive tasks by automatically adjusting its tone based on the recipient's gender. It tends to use Pathos (emotional) and communal language for women, while favoring Logos (rational) and logical arguments for men.

View Detailed Analysis →

Discovery 2: Recruitment Fairness Experiment

In explicit recruitment scoring tasks, DeepSeek-V3 demonstrated strong "self-correction" capabilities. When evaluating candidates with identical technical qualifications (Python/SQL) but different social attributes, the model consistently issued a score of 8.0/10, effectively neutralizing potential discrimination.

View Detailed Analysis →

Discovery 3: Technical Interventions

The study highlights that Prompt Engineering is a vital mechanism for achieving equity. Techniques like Adversarial Prompting can reduce bias by approximately 80%. Furthermore, model architecture is crucial; for instance, Claude’s "Constitutional AI" provides an inherent defensive advantage.
