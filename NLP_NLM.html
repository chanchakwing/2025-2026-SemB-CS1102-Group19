Neural Language Model (NLM)

Refer to the Research Team, Wang et al. (2024) state that The introduction of Deep Learning (RNN, LSTM) shifted the paradigm.

Mechanism: NLM uses Word Embeddings to reflect words into a high-dimensional semantic vector space. The position of a word in this space is determined by its context and usage in history.

Core Concept: The closer two words are in this vector space, the more related they are. This allowed computers to finally "understand" meaning through mathematical distance.

Social Equity Insight: become Associative. Stereotypes are hidden in the geometry of the vector space (e.g., the vector for "homemaker" being closer to "woman" than "man")
