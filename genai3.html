What are Foundation Models?

Generative AI is built upon massive deep learning structures known as Foundation Models. These represent a paradigm shift in how AI systems are developed and deployed.

Key Characteristics:

  • Scale: These models feature an immense number of parameters, allowing them to capture complex nuances in data.
  • Pre-training: They are trained on a vast and diverse range of datasets (text, images, code) before being specialized for specific tasks.
  • Versatility: A single foundation model can be adapted to power various applications, from translation and creative writing to image synthesis and data analysis.

In essence, a Foundation Model acts as a "central brain" that provides a base of knowledge and capability, which can then be fine-tuned for social equity, technical precision, or creative expression.

Descriptive Truth

Foundation Model produce to descriptive truth, which is statistical belief, It doesn't express "correctness," but rather"the status quo." It honestly reflects the statistical frequency of human behavior in online data.

Without Socialization：It doesn't understand "Social Hehavior Norms, "it only understands" statistical correlation.

FROM DESCRIPTIVE TRUTH TO NORMATIVE TRUTH

Public Relationship Disaster: Violation in Political Correctness, AI Firm will be banned to operate.

To avoid violate in the following aspects, the model need to be aligned with the society's expectation and value.

  • Political Correctness
  • Law & Regulation
  • DEI Principle: Stands for Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion
  • Society Value & Norm

Post training

To adjust the bias learn from data, make its output fulfill the society's expectation and value.

  • SFT (Small Instruction Finite Adjustment): Transforms the pre-trained model from a "word chain" into an "instruction executor." Its core lies in data quality, establishing a baseline strategy through a small number of high-quality expert dialogues.
  • RLHF (Reinforcement Learning): Endows the model with social emotional intelligence. It uses reward models to simulate human ratings and directly optimizes preferences through algorithms such as DPO, making the answers closer to human responses.
  • Ethical Challenges: Faces the trade-off of 3H (usefulness, honesty, harmlessness). Over-alignment can lead to "rejection bias" or even an "alignment tax," thereby reducing logical ability.
  • Continuous Safety: The risk of "forgetting" must be addressed after deployment. Anthropic's constitutional AI provides a constitutional solution for large-scale automated alignment by having AI supervise AI.
  • Output Guardrails: A monitoring and filtering system. This provides a final check before the answer is sent to the user. If it detects harmful content such as hate speech, pornography, violence, It immediately blocks it and replaces the answer with a standard rejection message


It help AI learn to navigate sensitive topics with tact and disclaimers to ensure corporate survival and public acceptance

Alignment Tax

The Cost of Forced Alignment: Alignment Tax
Force models to strictly adhere to PC, DEI, Social Norms, or other matters.
Leads to ' over-refusal' or evasive, roundabout behaviors.
Compromise the model’ s objective reasoning and its ability to present raw factual truths.

Why Need to Align the Foundation Models

Representation Issue: Cyber Place

Not total proportional mirroring the reality.
When AI firm scrapes data from the internet, it is not reading "the thoughts of all humanity, " but rather reading the traces left by "the most talkative people. "

  • Talkative people
  • Speaker of major language（Chinese, English）
  • Young People, Urbanites..
Have higher weight in the dataset than other groups.
  • Non-Talkative people:
  • Speakers of minority languages
  • The Elderly
Almost "invisible" in the dataset, pose to cognitive blind spots as the insufficient sample size.

Representation Issue: The Spiral of Silence

Self-censorship: People affraid to state different opinion against with Mainstream viewpoint, as they may be attacted.

Data contamination: The " statistical patterns " learned by AI are often only the loudest and most radical voices. If a bias is repeated ten thousand times online, even if it is wrong, AI will consider it a "high-probability truth. "
