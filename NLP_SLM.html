Statistical Language Model (SLM)

Refer to the Research Team, Wang et al. (2024) state that The journey began with models like N-gram. These models operate based on massive amounts of historical data provided by humans.

Mechanism: It relies on Next Word Prediction based purely on probability. By looking at historical records and Part of Speech (POS), the computer determines which word is most likely to follow the current sequence.

Limitation: The computer does not understand the semantic meaning of words; it is essentially a "stochastic parrot" counting occurrences.

Social Equity Insight: Biases in SLMs are Explicit. If a dataset contains 80% "He" following "Doctor," the model simply mirrors this statistical imbalance
